# M8 Fully allocated profitability

**Method.** Five methods: revenue-based, headcount-based, activity-based (driver shares), step-down (fixed order), reciprocal (simultaneous equations T = (I - A^T)^-1 D). Rank sensitivity across methods; monthly HTML profitability pack.

**Outputs.** `fact_allocation`, `mart_profitability_by_business`, `mart_allocation_rank_sensitivity`, `outputs/reports/profitability_pack_202606.html`.

**Results and validation.** Property tests: every method conserves total pool cost; reciprocal equals direct when there is no inter-pool usage; fully allocated pre-tax reconciles to the public segment pre-tax. Result: 4 of 6 businesses change rank by method; Liquidity Solutions +36.4% margin (revenue basis) vs -20.1% (activity-based), rank 2 to 6, with positive contribution margin.

**Limitations.** Business split and drivers are synthetic. Monthly pack phases revenue evenly within the quarter (public revenue is quarterly).
